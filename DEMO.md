## Script

### 0:00–0:10 — Hook

**Say:** “We know how Romeo and Juliet ends. Infinite Cinema asks a smaller, more cinematic question: what if Juliet woke up just five seconds earlier?”

**Show:** Title, question, and shared setup.

### 0:10–0:28 — One moment, two timelines

**Say:** “Both timelines share the same characters, lighting, camera language, and setup. Only one causal event changes.”

**Show:** Play the shared shot, then switch briefly between **Original tragedy** and **What-if timeline**.

### 0:28–0:45 — Explainability

**Say:** “This is more than a visual remix. Gemini analyzes the Scene DNA, preserves continuity, and explains the alternate outcome in exactly three beats.”

**Show:** Scroll through Scene DNA and the three beats.

### 0:45–1:02 — Agent architecture

**Say:** “Our full ADK director runs on Vertex AI Agent Engine. It researches the public-domain canon with Parallel, constructs the timeline, and produces an approved render manifest. Veo stays off until the owner explicitly approves the cost.”

**Show:** The architecture or ‘Under the Scene’ section. Do not start a Veo render.

### 1:02–1:20 — AI Audience

**Say:** “Then an AI Audience evaluates the result from three perspectives: emotional payoff, story causality, and visual continuity. Their consensus makes the creative result immediately legible.”

**Show:** Load the verified demo and reveal the overall score, consensus, and three persona verdicts.

### 1:20–1:30 — Close

**Say:** “Infinite Cinema turns one precise what-if into a grounded, explainable, and cost-controlled alternate timeline—and this same system can expand to any public-domain story.”

**Show:** Original/alternate comparison one final time.
